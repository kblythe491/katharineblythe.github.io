Your archetype

The kit is built around how you actually worked.

The quiz identifies which of the six Invisible Work archetypes best describes your career break. The bullet banks in this kit are organized by archetype — so you go straight to the language that fits your experience, not someone else's.

Builder

You created things from nothing. Launched, organized, built from scratch.

Operator

You kept complex systems running. Coordination, logistics — nothing fell through.

Implementer

You executed. Reliably, consistently, at volume. You were the one who did it.

Mentor

You developed people. Teaching, coaching, guiding growth over time.

Advocate

You navigated systems for someone who needed you. You made the case and kept pushing until there was an outcome.

Maker

You created content, experiences, or products. Creative output with real results.
